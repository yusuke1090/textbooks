# Appendix A. Glossary (English and Japanese)

## Rice

| English | Japanese | Reading | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Paddy, rough rice | 籾 | momi | Rice seed as harvested, with the hull still on |
| Brown rice | 玄米 | genmai | Rice with the hull removed. Trade amounts and prices are counted in brown rice |
| White rice, polishing | 精米 | seimai | White rice with the bran and germ of brown rice ground off. Also the act of grinding |
| Bale | 俵 | hyō | A unit of rice: 60 kg of brown rice |
| 10 ares | 10a | jū āru | 1,000 m² (one *tan*). Yield is given as "so many kg per 10 a" |
| Salt-water selection | 塩水選 | ensuisen | Put seed in salt water and keep only the heavy seeds that sink |
| Hot-water disinfection | 温湯消毒 | ontō shōdoku | Soak seed in 60 °C water for 10 minutes to prevent seed-borne disease |
| Soaking, sprouting | 浸種・催芽 | shinshu, saiga | Let the seed absorb water; make it sprout just a little |
| Raising seedlings | 育苗 | ikubyō | Growing seedlings, using seedling trays |
| Young, middle-aged and dense seedlings | 稚苗・中苗・密苗 | chibyō, chūbyō, mitsubyō | Types of seedlings by size and how they are raised |
| Puddling | 代かき | shirokaki | Stirring and leveling the soil of a flooded paddy |
| Rooting, establishment | 活着 | katchaku | Transplanted seedlings taking root |
| Leaf age | 葉齢 | yōrei | The number of leaves on the main culm. A guide to growth |
| Tillering | 分げつ | bungetsu | New stems coming out from the base of a stem |
| Productive tillers, unproductive tillers | 有効茎・無効分げつ | yūkōkei, mukō bungetsu | Stems that produce ears; stems that end without ears |
| Midseason drainage | 中干し | nakaboshi | Draining and drying the paddy partway through the season |
| Intermittent irrigation | 間断かんがい | kandan kangai | Repeatedly letting water in and letting it drain |
| Panicle initiation stage | 幼穂形成期 | yōsui keiseiki | When the ear starts to form inside the stem. About 25 days before heading |
| Meiosis stage | 減数分裂期 | gensū bunretsuki | When pollen forms. 10–12 days before heading. Sensitive to cold |
| Heading | 出穂 | shussui | The ear coming out |
| Ripening, grain filling | 登熟 | tōjuku | Starch building up in the grain as the rice ripens |
| Ripened grain ratio | 登熟歩合 | tōjuku buai | The share of grains that filled properly |
| 1,000-grain weight | 千粒重 | senryūjū | The weight of 1,000 grains of brown rice (g) |
| Basal fertilizer, panicle fertilizer | 元肥・穂肥 | motogoe, hogoe | Fertilizer before planting; top-dressing when the ear is forming |
| Single-application fertilizer | 一発肥料 | ippatsu hiryō | Fertilizer that works for a long time from one application (controlled-release) |
| Lodging | 倒伏 | tōfuku | Rice or wheat falling over |
| Sterility | 不稔 | funen | Grains not filling |
| Chalky immature grains | 白未熟粒 | shiro mijukuryū | Rice grains turned chalky white by heat |
| Pecky rice | 斑点米 | hantenmai | Rice with black spots from being sucked by stink bugs |
| Hopperburn | 坪枯れ | tsubogare | Rice dying in round patches from planthopper damage |
| Single-application herbicide | 一発処理剤 | ippatsu shorizai | A herbicide that holds back many weeds for a long time with one application |
| Final drainage | 落水 | rakusui | Draining the paddy before harvest |
| Rack drying | はざかけ | hazakake | Hanging harvested rice on racks to dry in the sun |
| Hulling | 籾摺り | momisuri | Removing the hulls to make brown rice |
| Country elevator | カントリーエレベーター | kantorī erebētā | A large shared facility for drying and storing paddy rice |
| Provisional payment | 概算金 | gaisankin | A temporary payment JA makes to farmers at shipping time |
| Negotiated transaction price | 相対取引価格 | aitai torihiki kakaku | The price at which collectors sell to wholesalers |
| Acreage reduction, production adjustment | 減反・生産調整 | gentan, seisan chōsei | Reducing the area or amount of rice grown |
| Crop conversion | 転作 | tensaku | Growing other crops instead of rice in paddies |
| Stockpiled rice | 備蓄米 | bichikumai | Rice the government holds in case of poor harvests |
| Direct seeding | 直播 | jikamaki, chokuhan | Sowing seed directly into the paddy without raising seedlings |

## Upland field crops

| English | Japanese | Reading | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| The four main field crops | 畑作4品目 | hatasaku yon-hinmoku | Hokkaido's wheat, sugar beet, potatoes and beans |
| Crop rotation | 輪作 | rinsaku | Growing crops in turn, changing every year |
| Continuous cropping injury | 連作障害 | rensaku shōgai | Growth getting worse when the same crop is grown again and again |
| Paddy–upland rotation | 田畑輪換 | denpata rinkan | Switching land between paddy and upland field every few years |
| Three crops in two years | 2年3作 | ninen sansaku | A combination of rice, wheat and soybeans grown over two years |
| Waterlogging damage | 湿害 | shitsugai | Damage when roots sit in water and stop growing |
| Open ditch, underdrain | 明渠・暗渠 | meikyo, ankyo | A drainage channel dug in the ground; a drainage pipe buried underground |
| Autumn-sown, spring-sown | 秋まき・春まき | akimaki, harumaki | Sown in autumn and grown through winter; sown in spring |
| Vernalization | 春化 | shunka | Being exposed to winter cold so the plant becomes ready to flower |
| Treading on wheat | 麦踏み | mugifumi | Treading on wheat in winter to make it root firmly |
| Wheat harvest season | 麦秋 | bakushū | Early summer, when wheat ripens |
| Fusarium head blight | 赤かび病 | akakabi-byō | A disease of wheat ears. It produces mycotoxins |
| Pre-harvest sprouting | 穂発芽 | hohatsuga | Grains sprouting on the ear before harvest |
| Rhizobia, root nodule bacteria | 根粒菌 | konryūkin | Bacteria living in legume roots that turn nitrogen from the air into a usable form |
| Nitrogen fixation | 窒素固定 | chisso kotei | Turning nitrogen from the air into ammonia and the like |
| Inter-row cultivation, hilling | 中耕・培土 | chūkō, baido | Cultivating between ridges; piling soil around the base of plants |
| Short-day plant | 短日植物 | tanjitsu shokubutsu | A plant that flowers when the days get shorter (soybeans, rice and others) |
| Seed potato | 種いも | taneimo | A potato for planting. Inspected by the government |
| Light sprouting | 浴光催芽 | yokkō saiga | Exposing seed potatoes to light before planting to make them sprout |
| Cyst nematode | シストセンチュウ | shisuto senchū | A tiny worm that attacks potato and soybean roots. It stays in the soil for decades |
| Paper pot | ペーパーポット | pēpā potto | A seedling container of joined paper tubes. Used for sugar beet seedlings |
| Beet pulp | ビートパルプ | bīto parupu | What is left after extracting sugar from sugar beet. Cattle feed |
| Grain corn | 子実用とうもろこし | shijitsu-yō tōmorokoshi | Corn whose kernels alone are dried for compound feed |
| *Geta* measure | ゲタ対策 | geta taisaku | Direct payments for field crops. They fill the cost gap with foreign producers |

## Soil, fertilizer and smart agriculture

| English | Japanese | Reading | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| The three phases | 三相 | sansō | Solids, water and air in the soil |
| Crumb structure, aggregate structure | 団粒構造 | danryū kōzō | Small lumps of stuck-together soil particles. Good drainage and water retention |
| Humus | 腐植 | fushoku | The black substance formed when organic matter breaks down |
| CEC | CEC | shī-ī-shī | Cation exchange capacity. The soil's ability to hold onto nutrients |
| Andosol | 黒ボク土 | kurobokudo | Black soil formed from volcanic ash. Phosphate works poorly in it |
| Magnesium | 苦土 | kudo | Magnesium (as a fertilizer term) |
| Green manure | 緑肥 | ryokuhi | A crop grown to be plowed into the soil |
| Soil testing | 土壌診断 | dojō shindan | Taking soil and measuring its nutrients and pH |
| GNSS | GNSS | jī-enu-esu-esu | Systems that measure position with satellites, such as GPS |
| RTK | RTK | āru-tī-kē | A method that measures position to the centimeter using base station information |
| Auto-steering | 自動操舵 | jidō sōda | A device that turns the steering wheel automatically to follow a set path |
| Variable-rate fertilizing | 可変施肥 | kahen sehi | Changing the amount of fertilizer spread by location |
| NDVI | NDVI | enu-dī-bui-ai | A number showing how healthy plants are from leaf color |
| LPWA | LPWA | eru-pī-daburyū-ē | Low-power wireless that reaches far (LoRa and others) |
| J-Credit | J-クレジット | jei kurejitto | Greenhouse gas reductions certified by the government so they can be sold |
| Agricultural support service | 農業支援サービス | nōgyō shien sābisu | Companies that take on work such as drone spraying |
