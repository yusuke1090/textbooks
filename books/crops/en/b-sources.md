# Appendix B. Sources

How crops are grown differs by region, variety and that year's weather. The numbers in this textbook are rough national guides, so when actually growing crops, check the prefecture's **cultivation guidelines**, JA's **spray calendar** and information from the agricultural extension center.

Rice prices and policy developments were researched on the following pages in November 2026. Most sources are in Japanese.

## Rice volumes and prices (Chapters 1 and 6)

- Harvest of the 2025 crop: [Hokkaido Shimbun (MAFF announcement)](https://www.hokkaido-np.co.jp/article/1288941/), [MAFF Tokai Regional Agricultural Administration Office (crop conditions)](https://www.maff.go.jp/tokai/press/tokei/251212.html), [MAFF Kanto Regional Agricultural Administration Office (harvest on a sieve-width basis)](https://www.maff.go.jp/kanto/press/toukei/kikaku/251212.html)
- Production guide for the 2026 crop: [Akita Sakigake Shimpo (Kyodo News, October 2025)](https://www.sakigake.jp/news/article/20251031CO0091/), [Kumamoto Nichinichi Shimbun](https://kumanichi.com/articles/1919824)
- Stocks and falling prices: [Kumamoto Nichinichi Shimbun (2026)](https://www.kumanichi.com/articles/2008528), [Kumamoto Nichinichi Shimbun: rice falls into the 2,000-yen range for the first time in two years](https://kumanichi.com/articles/2037469), [NHK (October 2026)](https://news.web.nhk/newsweb/na/nd-20261023de64466)
