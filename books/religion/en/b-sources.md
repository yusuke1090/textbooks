# Appendix B. Sources

The figures were checked on the following pages in November 2026. Numbers of believers vary greatly with how they are counted (self-identification, censuses, reports by religious organizations). The text uses the most widely used survey (Pew's 2020 estimates) throughout, and says so when another survey is used.

## Believers worldwide (Chapters 1 and 2, and each chapter's overview)

- Overall: [Pew Research Center, "How the Global Religious Landscape Changed From 2010 to 2020" (June 2025)](https://www.pewresearch.org/religion/2025/06/09/how-the-global-religious-landscape-changed-from-2010-to-2020/)
- Chapters by religion: [Christians](https://www.pewresearch.org/religion/2025/06/09/christian-population-change/), [Muslims](https://www.pewresearch.org/religion/2025/06/09/muslim-population-change/), [Hindus](https://www.pewresearch.org/religion/2025/06/09/hindu-population-change/), [Buddhists](https://www.pewresearch.org/religion/2025/06/09/buddhist-population-change/), [Jews](https://www.pewresearch.org/religion/2025/06/09/jewish-population-change/), [the religiously unaffiliated](https://www.pewresearch.org/religion/2025/06/09/religiously-unaffiliated-population-change/)

## Figures by country (Chapter 2)

- Kenya: Kenya National Bureau of Statistics, "2019 Kenya Population and Housing Census"
- Malawi: National Statistical Office, "2018 Malawi Population and Housing Census"
- Brazil: IBGE, religion tables of the "Censo Demográfico 2022" (2025)
- India: Census of India 2011 (Hindu 79.8%, Muslim 14.2%, Jain about 4.45 million)
- Other countries: the Pew study above

## Christianity (Chapter 4)

- Number of Catholics: [Vatican News, "New data from Annuario Pontificio 2026 shows Catholics growing" (March 2026)](https://www.vaticannews.va/en/vatican-city/news/2026-03/new-data-from-annuario-pontificio-2026-shows-catholics-growing.print.html)
