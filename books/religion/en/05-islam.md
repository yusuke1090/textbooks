# 5. Islam

## Overview

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Believers | About 2 billion (2020). About a quarter of the world's population, and growing fastest |
| Main countries | Indonesia (about 240 million), Pakistan (about 230 million), India (about 210 million), Bangladesh (about 150 million). About 60% of believers live in Asia and the Pacific |
| God | Allah ("God" in Arabic). The same one God as in Judaism and Christianity |
| Prophet | Muhammad (around 570–632). Regarded as the last prophet to convey God's word. Abraham, Moses and Jesus are also honored as prophets |
| Scripture | The Qur'an (Koran). Regarded as the very words God conveyed to Muhammad, and read in Arabic. The "Hadith", records of Muhammad's words and deeds, are also important |
| Place of prayer | The mosque |
| Leader | The imam (who leads prayer), the ulama (scholars of Islamic law) |
| Holy places | Mecca (the Kaaba), Medina (the mosque with Muhammad's tomb), Jerusalem |

- "Islam" means "surrender (to God)" in Arabic, and "Muslim" means "one who has surrendered", that is, a believer

## Origins and history

| When | Event |
| --- | --- |
| Around 570 | Muhammad is born in Mecca on the Arabian Peninsula. He lives as a merchant |
| Around 610 | In a cave on Mount Hira near Mecca, he is said to receive God's word through the angel Jibril (Gabriel) |
| 622 | Persecuted in Mecca, he moves to Medina (the Hijra). This year is the start of the Islamic calendar |
| 630 | He takes control of Mecca and removes the idols from the Kaaba |
| 632 | Muhammad dies. Successors (caliphs) lead the community |
| 7th–8th centuries | Spreads across the Middle East, North Africa, Iran, Central Asia and as far as Spain |
| 680 | At the Battle of Karbala, Muhammad's grandson Husayn is killed. An important event for the Shia |
| 8th–13th centuries | Learning flourishes in Abbasid Baghdad (algebra in mathematics, medicine, astronomy; translation of Greek books) |
| From the 13th century | Spreads to Southeast Asia and Africa through merchants and mystics (Sufis) |
| 1299–1922 | The Ottoman Empire rules the Middle East, North Africa and Eastern Europe |
| 20th century | Independence from colonial rule and the discovery of oil. The Islamic Revolution in Iran in 1979 |

## What is believed (the six articles of faith)

| Belief | Contents |
| --- | --- |
| God (Allah) | The one God. Has no form, and no images are made |
| Angels | They convey God's word. Jibril and others |
| Scriptures | Books God gave: the Torah, the Gospels, and the last and complete scripture, the Qur'an |
| Prophets | Adam, Noah, Abraham, Moses, Jesus, and last of all Muhammad |
| The afterlife | After death, people are divided into heaven and hell at the Last Judgment |
| Divine decree | Everything happens by God's will |

## The five pillars

| Practice | Contents |
| --- | --- |
| Declaration of faith (shahada) | Reciting "There is no god but God, and Muhammad is the messenger of God" |
| Prayer (salat) | Praying five times a day (dawn, noon, afternoon, sunset, night) facing Mecca (the qibla). At noon on Friday, people gather at the mosque to pray |
| Almsgiving (zakat) | Giving part of one's wealth (usually 2.5%) for the poor |
| Fasting (sawm) | For the month of Ramadan, no food or water from sunrise to sunset |
| Pilgrimage (hajj) | Those who can make a pilgrimage to Mecca once in their life, at a set time. Nearly 2 million people gather each year |

## Sunni and Shia

| | Sunni | Shia |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Share | About 85–90% | About 10–15% |
| View | Accept the caliphs chosen by the community as rightful successors. Follow Muhammad's words and deeds (the Sunnah) | Hold that only Ali, Muhammad's cousin and son-in-law, and his descendants (the imams) are the rightful leaders |
| Main countries | Almost all countries: Saudi Arabia, Turkey, Egypt, Indonesia, Pakistan and others | Iran, Iraq, Bahrain, Azerbaijan. Also many in Lebanon |
| Events | | Ashura, mourning the death of Husayn. Pilgrimages to saints' tombs |

- It began as a dispute over "who is the successor". The teachings are almost the same, but it sometimes overlaps with political rivalries (Saudi Arabia and Iran, for example)
- There is also mysticism seeking union with God (Sufism), known for prayers with whirling dances and more

## Rules of daily life

| Rule | Contents |
| --- | --- |
| Halal (permitted) | Meat slaughtered in the prescribed way. Pork and things made from pigs (lard, gelatin and so on) are forbidden (haram). Alcohol is also forbidden |
| Clothing | Both men and women avoid showing too much skin. How far women wear a headscarf covering the hair (hijab) or clothing covering the whole body varies by country and person |
| Finance | Charging interest is forbidden. Instead of interest, "Islamic finance" shares the profits |
| Law | Islamic law (sharia), based on the Qur'an and Hadith. It covers marriage, inheritance, food and trade. How far it is used in national law varies by country |
| Left hand | Eating and passing things are done with the right hand |

## Festivals and the calendar

| Event | Contents |
| --- | --- |
| Ramadan | The month of fasting. After sunset, people eat with family and friends (iftar). Mosques are lively at night |
| Eid al-Fitr | The festival at the end of the fast. People put on new clothes, pray, eat feasts and visit relatives |
| Eid al-Adha (Festival of Sacrifice) | The festival of the pilgrimage month. Sheep or cattle are sacrificed, and the meat is shared with family, relatives and the poor |
| The Prophet's Birthday | Celebrates Muhammad's birth (some hold that it should not be celebrated) |

- The Islamic calendar follows the phases of the moon, so a year is about 354 days. Ramadan and the festivals come about 11 days earlier each year in the solar (Western) calendar
- In many countries Friday is a day off (Saudi Arabia and others take Friday and Saturday; Indonesia and Turkey Saturday and Sunday)

## Today

- It is the fastest-growing religion. With many children, it grew by about 350 million in 2010–20
- Most believers live not in Arab countries but in Asia. Muslims who do not speak Arabic are the majority
- In Europe and North America, numbers are growing with immigration
- Some extremist armed groups use the name of Islam, but almost all Muslims oppose violence. The assumption "Muslims = dangerous" is wrong and leads to discrimination
- In Japan too, workers and students from Indonesia and elsewhere are increasing, and so are mosques, airport prayer rooms and halal shops

## Summary

- The one God, Allah, and the last prophet, Muhammad. The scripture is the Qur'an
- The six articles of faith and five pillars. Prayer five times a day, fasting in Ramadan, pilgrimage to Mecca
- Most are Sunni; the Shia are numerous in Iran and elsewhere
- Halal, avoiding pork and alcohol. Most believers live in Asia
