# よく使うコロケーション | Common collocations
pose a threat | phr | 脅威をもたらす | be a danger | Rising sea levels pose a threat to coastal cities.
raise awareness | phr | 関心を高める | make people know more | The campaign aims to raise awareness of food waste.
have an impact on | phr | 〜に影響を与える | affect | Social media has an impact on how we think.
take measures | phr | 対策をとる | act to deal with something | The government must take measures to cut pollution.
take steps | phr | 手段を講じる | act to achieve something | Schools are taking steps to reduce bullying.
play a key role | phr | 重要な役割を果たす | be very important | Farmers play a key role in protecting the countryside.
meet the demand | phr | 需要を満たす | supply what people want | Farms cannot meet the demand for organic milk.
address an issue | phr | 問題に取り組む | deal with a problem | We need to address the issue of rising rents.
reach a consensus | phr | 合意に達する | come to agreement | The committee failed to reach a consensus.
make a decision | phr | 決定を下す | decide | It is hard to make a decision without data.
make progress | phr | 進歩する | improve or move forward | Scientists have made progress in cancer research.
make an effort | phr | 努力する | try hard | We should make an effort to recycle more.
make a contribution | phr | 貢献する | help achieve something | Volunteers make a valuable contribution to society.
do research | phr | 研究する | carry out research | She is doing research on cow behaviour.
do harm | phr | 害を与える | cause damage | Too much screen time can do harm to children's eyes.
do damage | phr | 損害を与える | cause harm | Storms did serious damage to the crops.
gain experience | phr | 経験を積む | get experience | Students gain experience through internships.
gain access to | phr | 〜を利用できるようになる | be able to use | Rural families gained access to the internet.
pay attention to | phr | 〜に注意を払う | notice carefully | Pay attention to the word limit.
draw attention to | phr | 〜に注意を向けさせる | make people notice | The report draws attention to child poverty.
draw a conclusion | phr | 結論を導く | decide after considering facts | It is too early to draw a conclusion.
set a goal | phr | 目標を立てる | decide on an aim | Set a goal of learning ten words a day.
achieve a goal | phr | 目標を達成する | reach an aim | The country achieved its goal of cutting emissions.
face a challenge | phr | 課題に直面する | have to deal with a difficulty | Small farms face many challenges.
solve a problem | phr | 問題を解決する | find an answer to a problem | Technology alone cannot solve the problem.
cause a problem | phr | 問題を引き起こす | create a difficulty | Heavy traffic causes problems for residents.
a growing problem | phr | 深刻化している問題 | a problem that is getting worse | Obesity is a growing problem.
a major concern | phr | 大きな懸念 | a serious worry | Air quality is a major concern in cities.
a heavy reliance on | phr | 〜への強い依存 | depending strongly on | There is a heavy reliance on imported food.
a widespread belief | phr | 広く信じられている考え | an idea many people hold | There is a widespread belief that cities are unsafe.
a significant proportion | phr | かなりの割合 | a large share | A significant proportion of food is wasted.
a wide range of | phr | 幅広い | many different | The course covers a wide range of topics.
a large number of | phr | 多数の | many (countable) | A large number of students work part-time.
a great deal of | phr | 大量の | much (uncountable) | Farming requires a great deal of time.
strong evidence | phr | 強い証拠 | clear proof | There is strong evidence that exercise helps.
serious consequences | phr | 深刻な結果 | very bad results | Climate change will have serious consequences.
long-term effects | phr | 長期的な影響 | results lasting a long time | The long-term effects of the drug are unknown.
rapid growth | phr | 急速な成長 | fast increase | The city has seen rapid growth.
steady increase | phr | 着実な増加 | regular rise | There has been a steady increase in demand.
sharp decline | phr | 急激な減少 | sudden fall | There was a sharp decline in sales.
heavy traffic | phr | 交通量が多いこと | a lot of traffic | Heavy traffic causes delays every morning.
heavy rain | phr | 大雨 | a lot of rain | Heavy rain flooded the fields.
strong opposition | phr | 強い反対 | serious disagreement | The plan met strong opposition from residents.
broad support | phr | 幅広い支持 | support from many people | The idea has broad support.
public opinion poll | phr | 世論調査 | a survey of what people think | A public opinion poll showed support for the tax.
take responsibility for | phr | 〜の責任を負う | accept that you must deal with | Parents should take responsibility for their children's diet.
take advantage of | phr | 〜を利用する | use an opportunity | Students should take advantage of the library.
take into account | phr | 考慮に入れる | consider | We must take costs into account.
come to the conclusion | phr | 結論に達する | decide after thinking | I have come to the conclusion that both views are valid.
bear in mind | phr | 心に留める | remember | Bear in mind that prices may change.
keep pace with | phr | 〜に遅れずについていく | develop at the same speed | Wages have not kept pace with prices.
run the risk of | phr | 〜の危険を冒す | risk | Farmers who use one crop run the risk of losing everything.
put pressure on | phr | 〜に圧力をかける | push to do something | Tourism puts pressure on local services.
place emphasis on | phr | 〜を重視する | give importance to | Schools place emphasis on exam results.
lay the foundation for | phr | 〜の基礎を築く | prepare the basis for | Early education lays the foundation for later success.
strike a balance | phr | バランスをとる | find a middle way | We must strike a balance between growth and the environment.
fulfil a need | phr | 必要を満たす | provide what is needed | Libraries fulfil an important need in the community.
widen the gap | phr | 差を広げる | make a difference bigger | Private tutoring widens the gap between rich and poor.
bridge the gap | phr | 差を埋める | reduce a difference | Free laptops can help bridge the gap.
set an example | phr | 手本を示す | behave in a way others should copy | Parents should set an example for their children.
