# 意見と論じ方（Writing Task 2） | Opinions and argument (Writing Task 2)
in my view | phr | 私の考えでは | I think | In my view, the benefits outweigh the drawbacks.
in my opinion | phr | 私の意見では | I believe | In my opinion, education should be free.
I firmly believe | phr | 私は強く信じる | I am sure | I firmly believe that every child deserves a good education.
it is often argued that | phr | 〜とよく主張される | many people say | It is often argued that zoos are cruel.
it is widely believed that | phr | 〜と広く信じられている | many people think | It is widely believed that money brings happiness.
there is no doubt that | phr | 〜は疑いない | it is certain that | There is no doubt that the internet has changed our lives.
it could be argued that | phr | 〜と論じることもできる | one possible view is | It could be argued that tourism does more harm than good.
proponents | n | 支持者 | people who support an idea | Proponents of nuclear power point to its low emissions.
opponents | n | 反対者 | people who oppose an idea | Opponents argue that the tax hurts the poor.
critics | n | 批判する人 | people who say what is wrong | Critics claim the policy has failed.
supporters | n | 支持者 | people who agree with something | Supporters of the plan say it will create jobs.
on the one hand | phr | 一方では | used to introduce one side | On the one hand, cars give us freedom.
in contrast | phr | 対照的に | showing a difference | In contrast, trains produce far less pollution.
conversely | adv | 逆に | in the opposite way | Conversely, some people prefer rural life.
in addition | phr | さらに | also | In addition, the scheme is cheap to run.
what is more | phr | そのうえ | also, and more importantly | What is more, it helps the environment.
for instance | phr | 例えば | for example | For instance, Denmark taxes sugary drinks.
a case in point | phr | 好例 | a good example | Japan is a case in point.
as a result | phr | その結果 | because of this | As a result, many farmers left the industry.
owing to | prep | 〜のために | because of | Owing to the drought, yields fell.
due to | prep | 〜のせいで | because of | The match was cancelled due to rain.
in conclusion | phr | 結論として | to end | In conclusion, both sides have valid points.
to sum up | phr | まとめると | in summary | To sum up, the advantages are greater.
overall | adv | 全体として | considering everything | Overall, the plan is a good one.
to a certain extent | phr | ある程度まで | partly | I agree to a certain extent.
partially agree | phr | 部分的に賛成する | agree with some parts | I partially agree with this statement.
outweigh the drawbacks | phr | 欠点を上回る | be greater than the disadvantages | The benefits clearly outweigh the drawbacks.
a double-edged sword | id | 諸刃の剣 | something with good and bad effects | Social media is a double-edged sword.
the root cause | phr | 根本的な原因 | the main underlying cause | Poverty is the root cause of many social problems.
in the long term | phr | 長期的には | over a long period | In the long term, renewable energy is cheaper.
in the short term | phr | 短期的には | over a short period | In the short term, prices may rise.
there is a growing tendency | phr | 〜する傾向が強まっている | more and more people do | There is a growing tendency to shop online.
a growing number of | phr | ますます多くの | more and more | A growing number of people work from home.
it is essential that | phr | 〜することが不可欠だ | it is necessary that | It is essential that children learn to swim.
should be encouraged to | phr | 〜するよう奨励されるべきだ | ought to be persuaded to | People should be encouraged to cycle.
it is the responsibility of | phr | 〜の責任である | it is the duty of | It is the responsibility of parents to teach manners.
government intervention | phr | 政府の介入 | action by the government | Government intervention is needed to control prices.
individual responsibility | phr | 個人の責任 | what each person must do | Health is partly a matter of individual responsibility.
the general public | phr | 一般の人々 | ordinary people | The general public was not consulted.
society as a whole | phr | 社会全体 | everyone together | Crime affects society as a whole.
from an economic perspective | phr | 経済的な観点から | considering money | From an economic perspective, the plan makes sense.
a compelling argument | phr | 説得力のある主張 | a very convincing reason | There is a compelling argument for free school meals.
a valid point | phr | 妥当な指摘 | a reasonable idea | Critics make a valid point.
I would argue that | phr | 私は〜と主張したい | my view is that | I would argue that the benefits are greater.
it is undeniable that | phr | 〜は否定できない | it is certainly true that | It is undeniable that technology has made life easier.
this essay will discuss | phr | 本稿では〜を論じる | this essay will examine | This essay will discuss both views.
a number of factors | phr | いくつかの要因 | several causes | A number of factors explain this trend.
weigh up | pv | 比較検討する | consider carefully | We need to weigh up the costs and benefits.
by doing so | phr | そうすることで | in this way | By doing so, cities can cut traffic.
