# 句動詞 | Phrasal verbs
bring about | pv | 引き起こす、もたらす | cause to happen | The internet brought about huge changes.
bring up | pv | 育てる、話題に出す | raise a child, or mention | She was brought up on a farm.
carry out | pv | 実行する | do a task or study | Researchers carried out a survey.
come across | pv | 偶然見つける | find by chance | I came across an interesting article.
come up with | pv | 思いつく | think of an idea | The team came up with a new design.
cut down on | pv | 減らす | reduce the amount of | I'm trying to cut down on sugar.
deal with | pv | 対処する、扱う | take action to solve | The council must deal with the litter problem.
do away with | pv | 廃止する | get rid of | Many schools have done away with uniforms.
draw up | pv | 作成する（計画などを） | prepare a plan or document | The government drew up a new plan.
end up | pv | 結局〜になる | finally be in a situation | Most plastic ends up in the sea.
figure out | pv | 理解する、解決する | understand or solve | I couldn't figure out how the machine worked.
fill in | pv | 記入する | complete a form | Please fill in this form.
find out | pv | 知る、調べる | discover a fact | We need to find out why sales fell.
get across | pv | 伝える、わからせる | make people understand | The advert gets its message across clearly.
get rid of | pv | 取り除く、処分する | remove or throw away | We should get rid of single-use plastic.
give up | pv | やめる、あきらめる | stop doing | He gave up smoking last year.
go through | pv | 経験する、調べる | experience something difficult, or examine | The country went through a long recession.
hand in | pv | 提出する | give work to a teacher | Hand in your essay by Friday.
hold back | pv | 妨げる、抑える | stop from progressing | Poor internet is holding back rural businesses.
keep up with | pv | 遅れずについていく | stay at the same level as | It is hard to keep up with new technology.
look after | pv | 世話をする | take care of | Grandparents often look after children.
look into | pv | 調査する | investigate | The police are looking into the matter.
look up to | pv | 尊敬する | admire | Children look up to sports stars.
make up for | pv | 埋め合わせる | compensate for | Nothing can make up for lost time.
pass on | pv | 伝える、うつす | give to another person | Parents pass on their values to children.
pay off | pv | 報われる、完済する | bring good results, or repay debt | All his hard work paid off.
point out | pv | 指摘する | mention, draw attention to | The report points out several problems.
put forward | pv | 提案する | suggest an idea | She put forward a new plan.
put off | pv | 延期する、やる気をなくさせる | delay, or discourage | Don't put off your homework.
put up with | pv | 我慢する | tolerate | Residents put up with noise from the airport.
rule out | pv | 除外する | decide something is not possible | The police have not ruled out murder.
run out of | pv | 使い果たす | have no more left | We are running out of time.
set up | pv | 設立する | establish | She set up her own company.
show off | pv | 見せびらかす | try to impress | He likes to show off his new car.
sort out | pv | 解決する、整理する | solve or organise | We need to sort out the problem quickly.
stand out | pv | 目立つ | be easy to notice | Her essay stood out from the rest.
take on | pv | 引き受ける、雇う | accept work, or employ | The farm took on two new workers.
take over | pv | 引き継ぐ、乗っ取る | take control of | Robots are taking over routine tasks.
take up | pv | 始める（趣味を）、占める | start a hobby, or use space | I took up cycling last year.
think over | pv | よく考える | consider carefully | Think it over before you decide.
turn down | pv | 断る、音を下げる | refuse | She turned down the job offer.
turn out | pv | 結局〜とわかる | prove to be | The plan turned out to be a success.
use up | pv | 使い切る | use all of | We have used up our water supply.
wear out | pv | すり減る、疲れさせる | become damaged by use | My shoes wore out quickly.
wipe out | pv | 全滅させる | destroy completely | The disease wiped out the herd.
work out | pv | 解決する、算出する、運動する | solve, calculate, or exercise | Can you work out the total cost?
catch up with | pv | 追いつく | reach the same level | Developing countries are catching up with rich ones.
check in | pv | チェックインする | register at a hotel or airport | We checked in at the hotel at three.
drop off | pv | 降ろす、減る | take someone somewhere, or decrease | Sales dropped off in winter.
fall behind | pv | 遅れをとる | fail to keep up | Students who miss classes fall behind.
get by | pv | 何とかやっていく | manage with little | It is hard to get by on a small pension.
go ahead | pv | 進める、実施される | proceed | The project will go ahead next year.
hold on | pv | 待つ、持ちこたえる | wait | Hold on a minute, please.
kick off | pv | 始まる、始める | start | The festival kicks off on Friday.
look back on | pv | 振り返る | think about the past | I look back on my school days with pleasure.
pick up | pv | 身につける、拾う | learn without trying, or collect | I picked up some Spanish on holiday.
run into | pv | 偶然会う、（問題に）ぶつかる | meet by chance, or meet a problem | The project ran into difficulties.
set off | pv | 出発する、引き起こす | start a journey, or cause | We set off early in the morning.
settle down | pv | 落ち着く | start a quiet life | He settled down and bought a house.
speak up | pv | 意見を言う、大きな声で話す | say what you think, or speak louder | Students should speak up in class.
step down | pv | 辞任する | leave an important job | The minister stepped down.
throw away | pv | 捨てる | get rid of rubbish | We throw away too much food.
try out | pv | 試してみる | test something | Farmers can try out new machines at the show.
cut back on | pv | 削減する | reduce spending or use | Families are cutting back on luxuries.
opt for | pv | 〜を選ぶ | choose | Many people opt for public transport.
stick to | pv | 守る、続ける | continue doing or following | It is hard to stick to a diet.
