# 話すときの言い方と慣用句（Speaking） | Spoken phrases and idioms (Speaking)
to be honest | phr | 正直に言うと | saying what you really think | To be honest, I'm not much of a cook.
as far as I know | phr | 私の知る限りでは | based on what I know | As far as I know, the shop opens at nine.
as far as I'm concerned | phr | 私に関して言えば | in my opinion | As far as I'm concerned, health is more important than money.
I'd say | phr | 〜だと思う | I think | I'd say it's a bit of both.
on balance | phr | 全体として考えると | after considering everything | On balance, I think technology has made life easier.
it depends on | phr | 〜による | is decided by | It depends on the weather.
that said | phr | とはいえ | however | That said, I don't think farmers will disappear.
having said that | phr | そうは言っても | despite what I just said | It's expensive. Having said that, it's worth it.
to some extent | phr | ある程度は | partly | I agree with that to some extent.
by and large | phr | 概して | generally | By and large, people here are friendly.
all in all | phr | 全体的に見て | considering everything | All in all, it was a good experience.
in a nutshell | phr | 要するに | in a few words | In a nutshell, it's about time management.
let me think | phr | ちょっと考えさせて | I need a moment | Let me think about that for a moment.
that's a good question | phr | いい質問ですね | used to gain time | That's a good question. I've never really thought about it.
I suppose | phr | たぶん〜だと思う | I think, but I'm not sure | I suppose it's because people are busier.
I reckon | phr | 〜だと思う（くだけた言い方） | I think | I reckon prices will keep rising.
not much of a | phr | あまり〜ではない | not very good at or keen on | I'm not much of a sports fan.
get the hang of | phr | こつをつかむ | learn how to do something | It took a while, but I got the hang of it.
a piece of cake | id | 朝飯前、簡単なこと | very easy | The first exam was a piece of cake.
on the go | id | 忙しく動き回って | very busy | I'm always on the go at work.
once in a blue moon | id | めったに〜ない | very rarely | I go to the cinema once in a blue moon.
over the moon | id | 大喜びで | very happy | I was over the moon when I passed.
under the weather | id | 体調がすぐれない | slightly ill | I'm feeling a bit under the weather.
hit the books | id | 猛勉強する | study hard | I need to hit the books before the exam.
learn the ropes | id | 仕事のやり方を覚える | learn how to do a job | It took me a month to learn the ropes.
a breath of fresh air | id | 新鮮なもの、気分転換 | something new and pleasant | The new teacher was a breath of fresh air.
cost an arm and a leg | id | 非常に高くつく | be very expensive | The tickets cost an arm and a leg.
in the long run | id | 長い目で見れば | over a long period | In the long run, the investment will pay off.
the best of both worlds | id | 両方のいいとこどり | the advantages of two things | Working from home gives me the best of both worlds.
keep in touch | phr | 連絡を取り合う | stay in contact | I keep in touch with friends online.
make ends meet | id | 何とかやりくりする | have just enough money | Many families struggle to make ends meet.
get on well with | phr | 〜と仲がよい | have a good relationship with | I get on well with my colleagues.
look forward to | phr | 〜を楽しみにする | feel happy about something in the future | I'm looking forward to the holidays.
can't stand | phr | 〜が大嫌い、我慢できない | dislike very much | I can't stand crowded trains.
be into | phr | 〜にはまっている | be very interested in | I'm really into photography at the moment.
be keen on | phr | 〜が好き、熱心な | like or be interested in | I'm not very keen on spicy food.
these days | phr | 最近は | nowadays | These days, most people shop online.
nowadays | adv | 今日では | at the present time | Nowadays, few people write letters.
back in the day | phr | 昔は | in the past | Back in the day, we played outside all the time.
when I was a kid | phr | 子どものころ | in my childhood | When I was a kid, I spent summers on my grandfather's farm.
off the top of my head | id | 今すぐ思いつく限りでは | without checking | Off the top of my head, I'd say about fifty.
it's not my cup of tea | id | 私の好みではない | not something I like | Opera is not my cup of tea.
broadly speaking | phr | 大まかに言えば | in general | Broadly speaking, the city is safe.
generally speaking | phr | 一般的に言えば | usually | Generally speaking, people here are polite.
as a matter of fact | phr | 実のところ | actually | As a matter of fact, I've never been abroad.
what I mean is | phr | つまり言いたいのは | used to explain | What I mean is, it depends on the situation.
in other words | phr | 言いかえれば | to say it differently | In other words, the plan failed.
for one thing | phr | ひとつには | the first reason is | For one thing, it's too expensive.
the thing is | phr | 実は、問題は | the important point is | The thing is, I don't have much free time.
believe it or not | phr | 信じられないかもしれないが | used before surprising news | Believe it or not, I've never seen snow.
if I had to choose | phr | どちらか選ぶなら | if I must make a choice | If I had to choose, I'd pick the countryside.
I've never really thought about it | phr | あまり考えたことがない | used to gain time | I've never really thought about it, but I suppose so.
it goes without saying | phr | 言うまでもなく | it is obvious | It goes without saying that safety comes first.
time flies | id | 時が経つのは早い | time passes quickly | Time flies when you're having fun.
a means to an end | phr | 目的のための手段 | something done only to achieve something else | For me, work is a means to an end.
on the other hand | phr | 一方で | used to give the other side | On the other hand, city life is stressful.
a big fan of | phr | 〜の大ファン | someone who likes something a lot | I'm a big fan of Japanese food.
from time to time | phr | ときどき | occasionally | I go hiking from time to time.
every now and then | phr | ときどき | sometimes | Every now and then, I cook for my friends.
