# 説明と評価の形容詞 | Adjectives for describing and evaluating
significant | adj | 重要な、かなりの | important or large | There has been a significant rise in house prices.
substantial | adj | かなりの、相当な | large in amount | The project received substantial funding.
considerable | adj | かなりの | quite large | It took a considerable amount of time.
crucial | adj | 極めて重要な | extremely important | Early diagnosis is crucial.
vital | adj | 不可欠な | absolutely necessary | Clean water is vital for health.
essential | adj | 必要不可欠な | completely necessary | A good night's sleep is essential.
fundamental | adj | 根本的な | basic and important | There is a fundamental difference between the two systems.
key | adj | 主要な、鍵となる | most important | Education is a key factor in reducing poverty.
major | adj | 主要な、大きな | large or important | Traffic is a major cause of pollution.
minor | adj | 小さな、重要でない | small or not important | There were only minor changes to the plan.
primary | adj | 主な、最初の | main, or first | The primary aim is to reduce costs.
secondary | adj | 二次的な | less important | Price is a secondary concern for many buyers.
widespread | adj | 広まった | found in many places | There is widespread support for the plan.
prevalent | adj | 広く見られる | common at a particular time | Obesity is more prevalent in cities.
common | adj | よくある、共通の | happening often | It is common for students to work part-time.
rare | adj | まれな | not happening often | Such cases are extremely rare.
typical | adj | 典型的な | usual | A typical day starts at 5 a.m.
conventional | adj | 従来の、型どおりの | traditional or ordinary | Conventional farming relies on chemicals.
traditional | adj | 伝統的な | following old customs | Traditional crafts are disappearing.
contemporary | adj | 現代の | belonging to the present time | Contemporary art can be hard to understand.
modern | adj | 現代の、最新の | of the present time | Modern technology has changed farming.
innovative | adj | 革新的な | new and original | The company is known for its innovative products.
novel | adj | 新しい、斬新な | new and unusual | Researchers proposed a novel solution.
radical | adj | 根本的な、急進的な | very new and different | The system needs radical reform.
drastic | adj | 思い切った、徹底的な | extreme and sudden | Drastic action is needed to cut emissions.
gradual | adj | 徐々の | happening slowly | There was a gradual improvement in her health.
rapid | adj | 急速な | very fast | The city has seen rapid growth.
steady | adj | 着実な | regular and even | The company has shown steady growth.
sudden | adj | 突然の | happening quickly without warning | There was a sudden change in the weather.
dramatic | adj | 劇的な | sudden and very noticeable | The internet caused dramatic changes in society.
marginal | adj | わずかな | very small | There was only a marginal difference.
negligible | adj | ごくわずかな | too small to matter | The risk is negligible.
adequate | adj | 十分な | good or large enough | Many schools lack adequate funding.
sufficient | adj | 十分な | enough | There is not sufficient evidence.
insufficient | adj | 不十分な | not enough | Insufficient sleep affects concentration.
excessive | adj | 過度の | too much | Excessive screen time can harm children's eyes.
inevitable | adj | 避けられない | certain to happen | Some job losses are inevitable.
feasible | adj | 実行可能な | possible to do | It is not feasible to build a new road.
viable | adj | 実現可能な、成り立つ | able to work successfully | Small farms are no longer economically viable.
practical | adj | 実用的な、現実的な | useful or sensible | We need practical solutions.
realistic | adj | 現実的な | sensible and possible | Set realistic goals.
ambitious | adj | 野心的な | needing a lot of effort to succeed | The plan is very ambitious.
effective | adj | 効果的な | producing the intended result | Vaccines are an effective way to prevent disease.
efficient | adj | 効率的な | working well without waste | LED bulbs are more efficient than old bulbs.
productive | adj | 生産的な | producing a lot | Workers are more productive after a break.
beneficial | adj | 有益な | helpful | Regular exercise is beneficial to health.
detrimental | adj | 有害な | harmful | Smoking is detrimental to health.
harmful | adj | 有害な | causing harm | Harmful chemicals were found in the river.
adverse | adj | 不利な、悪い | negative or harmful | The drug has few adverse effects.
positive | adj | 前向きな、良い | good or helpful | The change had a positive effect on morale.
negative | adj | 否定的な、悪い | bad or harmful | Noise has a negative impact on sleep.
favourable | adj | 好ましい、有利な | good, or showing approval | The weather was favourable for the harvest.
reliable | adj | 信頼できる | able to be trusted | Public transport here is cheap and reliable.
accurate | adj | 正確な | correct in every detail | The sensor gives accurate readings.
precise | adj | 精密な、正確な | exact | We need precise measurements.
valid | adj | 妥当な、有効な | reasonable, or legally acceptable | That is a valid point.
relevant | adj | 関係のある | connected to the subject | Only include relevant information.
appropriate | adj | 適切な | suitable | Jeans are not appropriate for an interview.
suitable | adj | 適した | right for a purpose | This land is suitable for growing rice.
reasonable | adj | 妥当な、手ごろな | fair, or not too expensive | The prices are reasonable.
rational | adj | 合理的な | based on reason | There is no rational explanation.
logical | adj | 論理的な | reasonable and clear | The essay follows a logical order.
coherent | adj | 筋の通った | clear and well organised | She gave a coherent argument.
consistent | adj | 一貫した | always the same | His results have been consistent.
inconsistent | adj | 一貫しない、矛盾した | changing or not agreeing | The witness's story was inconsistent.
complex | adj | 複雑な | having many parts | Climate change is a complex issue.
complicated | adj | 込み入った | difficult to understand | The rules are too complicated.
straightforward | adj | 簡単な、率直な | easy to understand | The process is fairly straightforward.
sophisticated | adj | 高度な、洗練された | advanced and complex | The robot uses sophisticated sensors.
crude | adj | 粗い、大ざっぱな | simple and not exact | This is only a crude estimate.
diverse | adj | 多様な | very different from each other | London has a diverse population.
various | adj | さまざまな | several different | There are various ways to solve this.
numerous | adj | 多数の | many | The plan has numerous advantages.
abundant | adj | 豊富な | more than enough | The region has abundant water.
scarce | adj | 不足した、乏しい | not enough | Jobs are scarce in rural areas.
limited | adj | 限られた | small in amount | We have limited time.
vast | adj | 広大な、膨大な | extremely large | A vast amount of data is collected.
enormous | adj | 巨大な | very large | The project was an enormous success.
massive | adj | 巨大な | very big | There has been a massive increase in online shopping.
tiny | adj | ごく小さい | very small | Only a tiny fraction of plastic is recycled.
overall | adj | 全体の | including everything | The overall cost was lower than expected.
general | adj | 一般的な、全体の | not detailed, or for most people | The general public was not informed.
specific | adj | 特定の、具体的な | particular and exact | Give a specific example.
particular | adj | 特定の、特別な | special or individual | This is a particular problem in cities.
explicit | adj | 明確な | clearly stated | The rules are explicit.
implicit | adj | 暗黙の | suggested but not stated | There was an implicit threat in his words.
obvious | adj | 明らかな | easy to see | The answer is obvious.
evident | adj | 明白な | clearly seen | It is evident that the policy has failed.
apparent | adj | 明らかな、見かけ上の | clear, or seeming | For no apparent reason, sales fell.
potential | adj | 潜在的な、可能性のある | possible in the future | The plan has potential risks.
likely | adj | ありそうな | probable | It is likely that prices will rise.
unlikely | adj | ありそうにない | not probable | It is unlikely to rain today.
controversial | adj | 議論を呼ぶ | causing disagreement | Animal testing is a controversial issue.
debatable | adj | 議論の余地がある | not certain, open to argument | Whether it works is debatable.
questionable | adj | 疑わしい | doubtful | The results are questionable.
ethical | adj | 倫理的な | morally right | Is it ethical to keep animals in zoos?
moral | adj | 道徳的な | relating to right and wrong | We have a moral duty to help.
legitimate | adj | 正当な、合法の | reasonable or legal | Parents have legitimate concerns.
genuine | adj | 本物の、心からの | real and sincere | She showed genuine interest in the project.
artificial | adj | 人工の | made by people, not natural | The drink contains artificial sweeteners.
natural | adj | 自然の | existing in nature | Natural resources are being used up.
physical | adj | 身体の、物理的な | of the body, or real | Physical activity improves mental health.
mental | adj | 精神の | of the mind | Mental illness is still stigmatised.
emotional | adj | 感情の | of feelings | Pets give emotional support.
financial | adj | 財政の、金銭の | relating to money | Many students face financial difficulties.
economic | adj | 経済の | relating to the economy | Economic growth slowed last year.
social | adj | 社会の | relating to society | Unemployment causes social problems.
cultural | adj | 文化の | relating to culture | Festivals are part of our cultural heritage.
political | adj | 政治の | relating to politics | There is no political will to change.
environmental | adj | 環境の | relating to the natural world | Plastic causes serious environmental damage.
urban | adj | 都市の | relating to towns and cities | Urban areas are growing quickly.
rural | adj | 農村の、田舎の | relating to the countryside | Rural schools are closing.
domestic | adj | 国内の、家庭の | within a country, or the home | Domestic flights are cheap.
global | adj | 世界的な | relating to the whole world | Climate change is a global problem.
local | adj | 地元の | relating to a particular area | Buy food from local farmers.
national | adj | 国の、全国的な | relating to a whole country | It became a national issue.
international | adj | 国際的な | between countries | English is an international language.
public | adj | 公共の、公の | for everyone, or run by the state | Public opinion has changed.
private | adj | 私的な、民間の | for one person, or not run by the state | Private schools charge high fees.
compulsory | adj | 義務の、強制的な | required by law or rules | Education is compulsory until 16.
optional | adj | 任意の | not required | Attendance is optional.
temporary | adj | 一時的な | lasting for a short time | The job is only temporary.
permanent | adj | 永続的な | lasting for ever | The damage may be permanent.
long-term | adj | 長期的な | lasting a long time | We need long-term solutions.
short-term | adj | 短期的な | lasting a short time | Cutting taxes is only a short-term fix.
sustainable | adj | 持続可能な | able to continue without harm | We need sustainable sources of energy.
affordable | adj | 手ごろな値段の | not too expensive | There is a shortage of affordable housing.
