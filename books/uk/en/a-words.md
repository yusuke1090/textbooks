# Appendix A. English for farming and dairy

These are words you often hear from British farmers, in the news and at trade shows. Expressions that differ from American English are marked (UK).

## Fields and crops

| English | Japanese | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| arable | 畑作（の） | Growing crops in fields (as opposed to livestock) |
| combinable crops | コンバインで刈る作物 | Crops harvested with a combine (cereals, oilseed rape, beans) |
| winter wheat / spring barley | 秋まき小麦 / 春まき大麦 | Wheat sown in autumn / barley sown in spring |
| oilseed rape (OSR) | 菜種 | Rapeseed (UK). Called canola in America |
| sugar beet | てんさい | A root crop grown for sugar |
| drilling | 種まき | Sowing seed (the sowing machine is a drill) |
| tillage / min-till / no-till | 耕すこと / 少しだけ耕す / 耕さない | Working the soil / working it a little / not working it |
| cover crop | 被覆作物 | A crop planted to cover the soil while the field rests |
| yield (t/ha) | 収量 | Output, in tonnes per hectare |
| headland | 畑の端 | The edge of a field where machines turn |
| hedgerow / dry stone wall | 生け垣 / 石垣 | A living fence of shrubs / a wall of stacked stone without mortar |
| polytunnel | ビニールの簡易ハウス | A simple plastic-covered greenhouse |

## Livestock and dairy

| English | Japanese | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| dairy herd | 乳牛の群れ | A group of dairy cows on a farm |
| cow / heifer / calf | 雌牛 / 若い雌牛 / 子牛 | A female that has calved / a young female that has not calved yet / a young animal |
| bull / steer / bullock | 雄牛 / 去勢牛（米）/ 去勢牛（英） | An uncastrated male / a castrated male (US) / a castrated male (UK) |
| calving / block calving | 分娩 / いっせいの分娩 | Giving birth / having the whole herd calve within a set season |
| dry cow | 乾乳牛 | A cow resting, not being milked, before her next calf |
| milking parlour | 搾乳室 | The room where cows are milked (UK spelling; parlor in the US) |
| robotic milking / AMS | 搾乳ロボット | Automatic milking system |
| cubicle housing | フリーストールの牛舎 | A barn with a separate bed for each cow (UK) |
| grazing / paddock | 放牧 / 放牧地の区画 | Letting cattle eat grass in the field / a fenced section of pasture |
| silage / clamp | サイレージ / サイレージを詰める所 | Fermented forage / the place where it is packed (UK; a bunker) |
| slurry | ふん尿の液 | A liquid mixture of dung and urine |
| ppl (pence per litre) | 1リットルあたりのペンス | The unit for milk prices |
| milk recording | 乳検 | Testing each cow's milk yield and composition |
| SCC (somatic cell count) | 体細胞数 | A measure used to spot mastitis |
| bulk tank | バルククーラー | The tank that cools milk on the farm |
| mobility score / lameness | 歩様スコア / 跛行 | A score for how well a cow walks / walking badly because of foot or leg problems |
| BCS (body condition score) | ボディコンディションスコア | A score for how fat or thin a cow is |
| bTB (bovine TB) | ウシ結核 | Tuberculosis in cattle |
| ewe / ram / lamb / tup | 雌羊 / 雄羊 / 子羊 / 雄羊 | A female sheep / a male sheep / a young sheep / a male sheep (a northern English word) |

## Money and systems

| English | Japanese | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Defra | 環境・食料・農村省 | The Department for Environment, Food and Rural Affairs (England's farming ministry) |
| AHDB | 農業・園芸開発委員会 | The Agriculture and Horticulture Development Board (statistics and research) |
| NFU | 全国農業者組合 | The National Farmers' Union |
| BPS | 基本支払い | The Basic Payment Scheme (the EU-era area subsidy; ends in 2027) |
| ELM / SFI | 環境土地管理 / 持続可能な農業のインセンティブ | Environmental Land Management / Sustainable Farming Incentive |
| APR | 農業財産控除 | Agricultural Property Relief (inheritance tax relief) |
| aligned contract | スーパーと組んだ乳の契約 | A milk contract linked to a supermarket |
| Red Tractor | レッドトラクター | Britain's quality and safety assurance scheme for farm produce |
| TIFF (total income from farming) | 農業の所得の合計 | The total income of the farming industry |
| contractor | 作業を請け負う人 | Someone hired to do farm work (harvesting, spraying and so on) |

## Phrases you often hear

| English | Japanese | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| How's the herd doing? | 牛の調子はどう？ | A friendly question about the cows |
| We're out on grass. | 放牧に出している | The cows are out grazing |
| She's bulling. | 発情している | That cow is in heat |
| The milk cheque | 乳代 | The monthly payment for milk |
| It's been a wet one. | 雨の多い年だった | It has been a rainy year or season |
| Cheers / Ta | ありがとう | Thanks |
