# Appendix A. Swahili, Chewa and key terms

## Swahili (Tanzania, Kenya)

### Greetings

| Swahili | Pronunciation | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Habari? | ha-BA-ri | How are you? (hello). The answer is **Nzuri** (n-ZOO-ri, I'm fine) |
| Jambo | JAM-bo | Hello (a casual form used with tourists) |
| Mambo? | MAM-bo | Hey, what's up? (between young people). The answer is **Poa** (PO-a, cool) |
| Shikamoo | shi-ka-MO-o | A greeting to an elder. The answer is **Marahaba** (ma-ra-HA-ba) |
| Asante (sana) | a-SAN-te (SA-na) | Thank you (very much) |
| Karibu | ka-REE-bu | Welcome / you're welcome |
| Pole | PO-le | I'm sorry (to hear that); that must have been hard |
| Pole pole | PO-le PO-le | Slowly, slowly |
| Hakuna matata | ha-KU-na ma-TA-ta | No problem |
| Sawa | SA-wa | OK, got it |
| Ndiyo / Hapana | n-DEE-yo / ha-PA-na | Yes / no |
| Bei gani? | BAY GA-ni | How much? |
| Kwaheri | kwa-HE-ri | Goodbye |

## Key terms

| Term | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| AU | African Union |
| AfCFTA | African Continental Free Trade Area |
| EAC | East African Community (Kenya, Tanzania and others) |
| SADC | Southern African Development Community (Tanzania, Malawi and others) |
| COMESA | Common Market for Eastern and Southern Africa (Kenya, Malawi and others) |
| TICAD | Tokyo International Conference on African Development (held by Japan) |
| JICA | Japan International Cooperation Agency |
| M-Pesa | A system for sending and paying money by mobile phone (Kenya, Tanzania and others) |
| USSD | Menus used by dialing codes such as `*150*00#`. Works even on feature phones |
| PAYGo | Pay-as-you-go installments. Pay a little daily or weekly; the device stops if you do not pay |
| Informal | Business without registration or inspection. Such as milk that does not go through factories |
| Boda boda | Motorbike taxi |
| Zero-grazing | Keeping cattle in a shed without grazing, and bringing them cut grass |
| Zebu | Native cattle with a hump on the back. Tough against heat and disease |
| East Coast fever (ECF) | A cattle disease spread by ticks. Common in East Africa |
| Milk collection center | A place where farmers bring their milk to be cooled and sent to factories |
| MBG | Milk Bulking Group (Malawi) |
| KDB / TDB | Kenya Dairy Board / Tanzania Dairy Board |
| Cooling as a service | Renting coolers instead of selling them, and charging by use or by volume carried |
| IPC | An international scale that rates food crises in five phases. Phase 3 or above is "crisis" |
| Rain-fed agriculture | Farming with rain alone, without irrigation |
